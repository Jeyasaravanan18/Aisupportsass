SECTION 1: REFUND POLICY

1.1 Eligibility
Customers are eligible for a full refund if:
- Request is made within 14 days of purchase (annual plans)
- Request is made within 7 days of purchase (monthly plans)
- The product was not used extensively (fewer than 3 active sessions)

1.2 Non-Refundable Items
- Add-on services that have been fully delivered
- Training sessions that have been attended
- Custom integrations once development has begun

1.3 Process
Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. Customers must provide:
- Order/Invoice number
- Reason for refund
- Account email address

SECTION 2: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)

2.1 Response Times
| Priority | Description | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| P1 - Critical | System down, data loss | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| P2 - High | Major feature broken | 4 hours | 24 hours |
| P3 - Medium | Feature degraded | 8 hours | 72 hours |
| P4 - Low | General inquiry | 24 hours | 5 business days |

2.2 Priority Determination
Priority is set based on customer impact. Enterprise customers may escalate to P1 for any critical business impact. Support agents must not downgrade a customer's stated priority without manager approval.

---

SECTION 3: ESCALATION POLICY

3.1 When to Escalate
Agents must escalate to Level 2 when:
- Issue involves data loss or data corruption
- Customer has contacted support more than 3 times for the same issue
- Customer explicitly requests escalation
- Issue involves billing disputes over $500
- Customer expresses legal intent or mentions regulatory action

3.2 Escalation Process
1. Inform customer: "I'm escalating this to our senior team for priority handling."
2. Transfer ticket with full context notes
3. Ensure customer receives confirmation of escalation within 15 minutes
4. Follow up within 2 hours if no Level 2 response

3.3 De-escalation Techniques
When a customer is highly frustrated:
- Acknowledge their frustration: "I completely understand this has been frustrating."
- Avoid defensive language
- Focus on what CAN be done, not what can't
- Offer a concrete next step with a specific timeline
- If appropriate, offer a goodwill credit (up to $25 without manager approval)

---

SECTION 4: COMMUNICATION STANDARDS

4.1 Tone Requirements
- Use empathetic, professional language at all times
- Avoid jargon; use plain language
- Personalize responses using the customer's name
- End every interaction by confirming the issue is resolved

4.2 Prohibited Language
- "That's not possible" → Use "Here's what we can do instead..."
- "You should have..." → Use "Going forward, one option is..."
- "It's your fault" or implying customer error
- "I don't know" alone → Always follow with "Let me find out for you."

4.3 Closing Standards
Before closing a support interaction, agents must:
1. Confirm the customer's issue is fully resolved
2. Ask if there's anything else they can help with
3. Share relevant self-service resources
4. Provide the ticket/case number for reference

---

SECTION 5: COMPLIANCE AND DATA HANDLING

5.1 Customer Data
- Never access customer data without explicit permission
- Log all data access in the internal audit system
- Customer PII must not be shared in ticket summaries sent via email
- Screenshots of customer data require manager approval before sharing

5.2 GDPR / CCPA
- Honor data deletion requests within 30 days
- Provide data export within 30 days of request
- Direct customers to privacy@example.com for formal data requests

---

SECTION 6: GOODWILL AND COMPENSATION

6.1 Agent Authority
Support agents can offer without manager approval:
- Account credit up to $25
- 30-day plan extension
- One-time waiver of late fees

Level 2 agents can offer:
- Account credit up to $100
- 3-month plan extension
- Full refund for current billing period

6.2 Logging Requirements
All goodwill gestures must be logged in the CRM with:
- Reason for gesture
- Customer account ID
- Amount/type of compensation
- Approving agent ID
