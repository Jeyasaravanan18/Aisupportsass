ARTICLE: How to Reset Your Password
ID: FAQ-001
Category: Account Management

If you have forgotten your password or need to reset it, follow these steps:
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset email (check spam if not received within 5 minutes).
4. Click the link in the email — it expires after 24 hours.
5. Enter and confirm your new password. Password must be at least 8 characters and include one number.
6. Log in with your new password.

If you did not receive the email, verify the email address is correct, or contact support.

ARTICLE: Billing and Subscription Issues
ID: FAQ-002
Category: Billing

Common billing questions and solutions:

Q: Why was I charged twice?
A: Double charges are usually resolved within 3–5 business days. Share your transaction ID and we will investigate.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Account Settings → Subscription → Cancel Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

Q: How do I upgrade my plan?
A: Go to Account Settings → Subscription → Upgrade. You will be charged the pro-rated difference immediately.

Q: I need a refund.
A: Refunds are available within 14 days of charge for annual plans, 7 days for monthly. Contact support with your invoice number.

ARTICLE: App Not Loading / Performance Issues
ID: FAQ-003
Category: Technical Support

If the app is not loading or running slowly:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies (Ctrl+Shift+Delete on Chrome).
2. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
3. Disable browser extensions temporarily.
4. Check our status page at status.example.com for outages.
5. Ensure your internet connection is stable.
6. If on mobile, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

If the problem persists for more than 30 minutes, our team may be aware and working on it. Please submit a ticket with a screenshot.

ARTICLE: Data Export and Privacy
ID: FAQ-004
Category: Privacy & Data

How to export your data:
1. Go to Account Settings → Privacy → Export My Data.
2. Select the data types you want (messages, files, account info).
3. Click "Request Export". An email with a download link will be sent within 24 hours.

Your data is yours. We comply with GDPR and CCPA. You can request full account deletion at any time by contacting support@example.com.

ARTICLE: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Setup
ID: FAQ-005
Category: Security

Enable 2FA for extra account security:
1. Go to Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication.
2. Choose Authenticator App (recommended) or SMS.
3. For Authenticator App: scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or Authy.
4. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm setup.
5. Save your backup codes in a secure location.

If you lose access to your 2FA device, use a backup code. If you don't have one, contact support for identity verification.

ARTICLE: Integrations and API Access
ID: FAQ-007
Category: Integrations

We support integrations with:
